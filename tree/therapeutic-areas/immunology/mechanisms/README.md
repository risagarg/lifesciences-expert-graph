### Contents (6)
- [TNF inhibitors](tnf-inhibitors/README.md) · 33 experts — Block TNF-alpha. Drugs.com lists biosimilars for adalimumab, infliximab and etanercept.
- [IL-17 pathway](il-17-pathway/README.md) · 37 experts — Block IL-17A, IL-17A and IL-17F together, or the IL-17 receptor A.
- [IL-23 pathway](il-23-pathway/README.md) · 77 experts — Block IL-23 through its p19 subunit, the p40 subunit shared with IL-12, or the IL-23 receptor.
- [TYK2 inhibitors](tyk2-inhibitors/README.md) · 17 experts — Oral inhibitors of tyrosine kinase 2, which mediates IL-23, IL-12 and type I interferon signaling. One approved drug and two in phase 3.
- [PDE4 inhibitors](pde4-inhibitors/README.md) · 49 experts — Phosphodiesterase 4 inhibitors, in both oral and topical forms.
